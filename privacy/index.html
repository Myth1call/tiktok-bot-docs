Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2026

This policy describes how the "Twitch to TikTok Bot" software handles information when run locally by the account owner. There is no cloud backend operated by the developer for end-user data storage.

1. Data processed locally

  • Twitch clip metadata (clip ID, title, broadcaster name, game, duration).
  • Video files stored on your computer (e.g. storage/raw, storage/processed).
  • Job status in a local SQLite database on your device.

2. Credentials and tokens

  • Twitch and TikTok API credentials are stored in the OS credential store (Windows Credential Manager / keyring), not in public repositories.
  • TikTok OAuth access and refresh tokens are stored locally on your machine after you authorize the app.
  • The developer does not receive or host your tokens on a remote server.

3. What we do not do

  • We do not sell personal data.
  • We do not run third-party advertising or analytics trackers in this project.
  • We do not collect data from other users' TikTok or Twitch accounts beyond what you explicitly process via the APIs you authorize.

4. Sharing with third parties

When you use the software, data is sent only to services you configure: Twitch (Helix API), TikTok (Content Posting API), and your local FFmpeg processing. Those platforms have their own privacy policies.

5. Your choices

  • You can delete local video files and the SQLite database at any time.
  • You can revoke TikTok authorization in your TikTok account settings.
  • You can remove stored secrets from Windows Credential Manager.
